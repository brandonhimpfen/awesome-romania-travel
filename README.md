> A curated list of resources for traveling in Romania, covering official tourism boards, national rail networks, Transylvanian castles, Carpathian mountain reserves, and digital nomad options.

Romania is a fascinating Eastern European destination encompassing medieval Transylvanian fortresses, the Carpathian mountain range, painted monasteries, and the Danube Delta. This list is intended for cultural historians, medieval architecture explorers, mountain hikers, and eco-travelers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, Schengen visa rules, and weather forecasting.

- [Romania Tourism (Official Portal)](https://romania.travel/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination highlights, cultural routes, and regional itineraries.
- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visas](https://www.mae.ro/en/node/2035) - Official consular portal for Schengen visa entry regulations, passport guidelines, and diplomatic missions.
- [ANM Weather (National Administration of Meteorology)](https://www.anm.ro/) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and alpine climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway networks, intercity trains, long-distance buses, and urban transit.

- [CFR Călători (Romanian Railway Passenger Operator)](https://www.cfrcalatori.ro/en/) - National passenger rail network connecting Bucharest with major cities, regional hubs, and Carpathian mountain towns.
- [STB Bucharest Public Transport](https://stbsa.ro/) - Official public transit operator managing Bucharest's urban tram, trolleybus, and bus networks.
- [Metrorex Bucharest Subway](https://www.metrorex.ro/) - Official rapid transit authority operating the underground metro lines across the capital.
- [TAROM](https://www.tarom.ro/en) - National flag carrier operating international routes and domestic flights connecting Bucharest (OTP) with regional airports.

## Destinations & Regions

### Bucharest & Wallachia

- [Visit Bucharest (Official City Guide)](https://bucharest.travel/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the historic Lipscani district, Palace of the Parliament, and museum circuit.
- [Peles Castle Official Portal](https://peles.ro/en/) - Official visitor and ticketing guide for the stunning Neo-Renaissance royal castle nestled in Sinaia.

### Transylvania & Medieval Towns

- [Brasov Tourism Official Portal](https://www.brasovcity.ro/) - Official municipal travel guide for exploring Brasov's medieval old town, Council Square, and Mount Tampa cable car.
- [Bran Castle (Dracula's Castle) Official Site](https://www.bran-castle.com/) - Official visitor portal and ticketing guide for the historic fortress perched in the Carpathian foothills.
- [Sibiu / Visit Sibiu](https://www.sibiu.ro/) - Regional visitor guide for exploring the historic Saxon squares, Bridge of Lies, and Astra Open-Air Museum.
- [Sighisoara Tourism Portal](https://www.sighisoara.org.ro/) - Official guide for exploring the UNESCO-listed medieval citadel and Vlad Dracul's birthplace.

### Maramureș & Painted Monasteries

- [Maramureș County Tourism Council](https://maramures.travel/) - Official regional portal for traditional wooden churches, carved wooden gates, and preserved rural heritage.
- [Bucovina Painted Monasteries Guide](https://romania.travel/) - Official tourism resource for visiting the UNESCO-listed exterior frescoed monasteries of northern Moldavia.

### Black Sea Coast & Danube Delta

- [Danube Delta Biosphere Reserve Authority](https://www.ddbra.ro/) - Official environmental administration managing boat tours, birdwatching permits, and eco-tourism guidelines for Europe's best-preserved river delta.
- [Constanta Tourism Portal](https://primaria-constanta.ro/) - Official visitor guide for Black Sea coastal resorts, historic casino architecture, and ancient Tomis ruins.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Romania's Digital Nomad Visa framework.

- [Romania Digital Nomad Visa (General Inspectorate for Immigration)](https://igi.mai.gov.ro/en/) - Official government portal detailing income requirements, tax residency rules, and 1-year visa application procedures for non-EU remote workers.
- [TechHub Bucharest](https://www.techhub.com/) - Pioneer coworking space and community innovation hub located in central Bucharest.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, mountain rescue, and traveler safety.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.mai.gov.ro/) - Integrated single European emergency hotline for police (Poliția), fire brigade (Pompieri), and medical ambulance assistance.
- [Salvamont Romania (National Mountain Rescue)](https://salvamontromania.ro/) - Official mountain rescue and emergency response service operating patrols across the Carpathian ranges.
